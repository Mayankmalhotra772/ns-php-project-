IMPORTANT: NEVER use "docker compose down -v" — the -v flag deletes volumes and WIPES the database!
DB is stored in pg_data volume and survives rebuilds as long as you don't use -v.
Uploaded images are stored in upload_data volume and also survive rebuilds.
